What WildObserve™ does

Turn camera-trap folders into data ready for analysis.

WildObserve™ is a desktop app that turns folders of camera-trap images into reviewed data that's ready for analysis.

  • •

    Finds what’s in each image

    It marks animals, people, and vehicles, and flags images that look empty.

  • •

    Suggests the species

    Suggestions start from SpeciesNet and get better as your Project Model learns from your labels.

  • •

    Makes review fast

    Images are grouped into capture events, so you can name a whole sequence at once. You can filter by site, camera, date, or species, and record counts, sex, life stage, and behavior.

  • •

    Watches for new images

    Point it at a folder and it processes new images as they arrive. It can also send email alerts when a species you care about shows up.

  • •

    Exports your data

    It exports to CSV, Camtrap DP, Wildlife Insights, COCO, YOLO, and MegaDetector formats.
