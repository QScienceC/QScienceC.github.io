Plans

What each plan includes, and what it allows.

Every plan runs on your own computer: your images, their metadata and your annotations never leave it. Community is free. Researcher, Lab and Organization are annual licenses: their price stays the same for the year you've paid for, and when one ends, WildObserve™ carries on as Community with nothing lost. Commercial use requires an Organization license; the Terms of Service say exactly what counts.

Production Model Training

We use your complete set of labeled images to create a Production Model: an object-detection model made for your data, which detects, classifies and counts the animals in it. It's a separate service, priced by your dataset's size and readiness, under its own quote.
